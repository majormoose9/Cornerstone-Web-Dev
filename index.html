FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need to know anything about technology?
+
Not at all. Our whole process is designed for business owners, not tech people. You focus on describing your business and what you want — we handle every technical detail. Most clients are surprised by how simple it feels.
How long does it take to build my website?
+
Most websites are live within 14 days of our first conversation. The process: discovery call → we send you a design preview within 5 business days → you give feedback → we finalize and launch. Simple projects can go live even faster.
What if I already have a website I hate?
+
We rebuild it from scratch. We'll look at what's not working — whether it's the look, the content, the speed, or all three — and fix it. We can also migrate your domain and any content you'd like to keep.
Will I be able to update the website myself?
+
You don't have to — that's part of the service. Just text or email us what you need changed, and we'll handle it, usually within 24 hours. If you'd prefer to have login access to make changes yourself, we can set that up too.
Is there a contract? What if I want to cancel?
+
No contract at all. You pay once, we build your site, and you own it outright. There's no subscription to cancel and no recurring commitment. If you ever want ongoing help with updates, we can discuss that separately — but there's no pressure.
What areas do you serve?
+
We primarily work with businesses in the local area, and we genuinely enjoy meeting clients in person when possible. That said, we work remotely too — so if you've found us from outside the area, we're still happy to talk.
